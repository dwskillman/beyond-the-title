My name is Morgan Fixture and I'm a customer service specialist. I've been
answering calls for almost six years now, and I still learn something new
almost every day.

One call I'll never forget was from a customer who was trying to help her
father after he moved into assisted living. She had been transferred four
times and she was close to giving up. I stayed with her until every account
question was answered, and she cried when we finished because it was the
first thing that had gone right all week.

After that call I realized a lot of us were looking up the same answers again
and again. So I created a one-page quick reference guide for family caregiver
questions. My manager shared it with the whole floor, and now more than forty
colleagues use it every day.

I never thought a single page could make such a big difference. The best part
is that callers spend less time on hold, and we get to spend more time actually
listening to them.
